About the company

Five decades of national contracting

Founded in 1975 as MECON, Qatar Engineering & Construction Company (Qcon) has grown into the country's leading national EPC and maintenance contractor — contributing immensely to Qatar's hydrocarbon infrastructure across Oil & Gas, Petrochemical, Fertilizer and Power.

A multi-discipline industrial contractor, Qcon delivers total solutions through integrated Engineering, Procurement, Construction, Commissioning and long-term Maintenance — supported by its own fabrication yards, marine wharf, equipment fleet and accommodation facilities at Qatar's industrial hubs.

DIVISIONS
Services division

Six divisions. One contractor.

From greenfield EPC mega-projects to plant turnarounds and rapid-response maintenance — every discipline delivered under one integrated management system.

DIV — MPD

Major Projects

Integrated multi-discipline onshore & offshore EPC/EPIC and general construction — pipelines, tank farms, LNG storage, water treatment and power plants for national and regional markets.

DIV — MSD

Maintenance Services

Plant maintenance for static & rotating equipment, shutdowns & turnarounds, catalyst exchange, long-term support contracts and a Rapid Response Team for short-notice works.

DIV — OSD

Offshore Services

Marine fabrication yard with deep experience in onshore construction of offshore fixed platforms — topsides, jackets, large-bore spools, blasting & painting at the Mesaieed wharf.

DIV — CID

Civil & Infrastructure

Building construction and interior finishing experience dating back to 1974 — industrial buildings, foundations, roads and infrastructure as a Grade A classified civil contractor.

DIV — SSD

Support Services

Extensive in-house plant & equipment fleet including cranes up to 300-ton capacity, scaffolding services, technical manpower supply and equipment hire across all sites.

DIV — E&I

Electrical & Instrumentation

Grade 1 KAHRAMAA-licensed electrical contracting — comprehensive E&I engineering, installation, testing and commissioning for power and process facilities.
